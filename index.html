FAQ

Questions we hear often

What ERP software does Arvension Technologies offer?

We offer ready-to-customise ERP systems for construction, schools, colleges, hostels, libraries, mess and canteens, stores, microfinance, chit funds, gold loan, Nidhi companies and multi-state co-operative societies. Each one is available with mobile apps.

Are the ERPs AI-based?

Yes. Our ERPs include AI features such as risk prediction, smart reminders, anomaly detection and a plain-language assistant that answers questions about your data.

Do the finance ERPs have unlimited branches and staff?

Yes. Our microfinance, chit fund, gold loan, Nidhi and co-operative ERPs support unlimited branches, staff and customers.

Which mobile apps are included?

Depending on the ERP: a customer or member app, a field agent app with live GPS tracking, a staff attendance punch-in app, a parent app, a school bus driver app with live tracking and a special Director app for Nidhi and co-operative societies.

Can you customise the software for my business?

Yes. Every project starts with a requirement study, and we configure and customise forms, reports, workflows and branding to match how you work.

Where are you located and do you work with clients outside Kerala?

We are based in Technopark, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, and we serve clients across India. Demos, training and support are available online and on site.
