Overview

Our School Management System runs the whole school from one login: admissions, attendance, timetable, fees, exams and communication. The premium edition adds a Parent App and a Bus Driver App with punch-in and live GPS tracking, so parents always know where the school bus is and when their child boarded and got down.

Built for: CBSE, ICSE and State board schools, nursery and play schools, and school groups with multiple branches.

Features

What is inside the School Management System

  • Enquiry, admission and student records
  • Student and staff attendance (app, RFID or biometric)
  • Timetable and substitution
  • Fee structure, receipts and online payment
  • Exams, marks and report cards
  • Homework, circulars and notices
  • Transport routes, stops and bus allocation
  • Library and store
  • Hostel and mess integration
  • Staff payroll and leave
  • SMS, WhatsApp and push notifications
  • Certificates, TC and ID cards
Mobile apps

Mobile apps included

Parent Mobile App

Attendance, fees, homework, results, circulars, leave requests and live bus location, all in one app for parents.

Bus Driver App with Punch-in

Drivers start the trip, punch in at every stop and the school sees the bus live on the map. Parents get a notification when the bus is near.

Teacher & Staff App

Teachers mark attendance, enter marks, post homework and message parents from their phone.

FAQ

School Management System: common questions

Is the school bus tracking real-time?

Yes. The driver app sends the bus location continuously during a trip. The school office and parents see it live on a map.

Can we buy it without the mobile apps?

Yes. The web ERP can be used on its own and the Parent and Bus apps can be added whenever you are ready.

Does it support multiple branches?

Yes. A school group can manage many branches under one head office with branch-wise and consolidated reports.
