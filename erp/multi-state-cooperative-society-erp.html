One system for members, shares, deposits, loans and accounts across states, with mobile apps for members, agents, staff and the board.

Overview

A multi-state co-operative society has members, branches and rules spread across regions. Our ERP gives you one consolidated view of the whole society while each branch runs its day-to-day work, and all stakeholders, from members to directors, have an app that fits their role.

Built for: Multi-state co-operative credit and thrift societies, agricultural and farmers' societies and large co-operative organisations.

Features

What is inside the Multi-State Co-operative Society ERP

  • Member register, shares and nominee records
  • Deposits: savings, fixed, recurring and pigmy
  • Loans with configurable products and security
  • Multi-state, multi-branch hierarchy
  • Collection by branch staff and field agents
  • General ledger, trial balance and statements
  • Dividend and interest processing
  • Board, AGM and resolution records
  • Audit and statutory reporting support
  • HR, payroll and attendance
  • Notices, SMS and WhatsApp
  • Role-based access and audit trail
Mobile apps

Mobile apps included

Member Mobile App

Members check shares, deposits, loans, passbook entries and pay instalments.

Field Agent App with Live Tracking

Agents collect payments, register customers and follow their daily route, while the head office sees every agent's live GPS location.

Staff Attendance Punch-in App

Staff punch in and out from the phone with GPS and selfie verification, so branch attendance is accurate and tamper-proof.

Director / Board App

Directors review branch performance, approve items and receive alerts from anywhere.
