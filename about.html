A Kerala software team that builds the systems businesses run on

Arvension Technologies LLP started as a student start-up from College of Engineering Attingal (IHRD) and is today based in Technopark, Thiruvananthapuram.

Our story

From campus start-up to ERP specialists

We began by building software for small businesses and training students. Over time we saw the same problems everywhere: schools, finance companies and contractors running critical work on registers and spreadsheets.

So we built ERP systems for them, and wrapped each in the mobile apps real teams need: attendance, field collection with live tracking, parent and customer apps. Today we also build AI websites and chat apps, web apps and mobile apps for clients across India.
